Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Joyscroll need access to my Photos?

Joyscroll creates your personal feed from your own photo library. Without access, the app cannot function as intended.

What happens if I allow only limited photo access?

You can still use Joyscroll, but the feed will be limited to the photos you selected. For the full experience, grant access to all photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Joyscroll.

How do I relive Live Photos with sound?

Ensure your device is not muted and the volume is up. Joyscroll automatically plays the motion and audio from your Live Photos.

How are ads shown?

Ads are displayed occasionally in the feed to support development. They are designed to be minimal and non-intrusive.
