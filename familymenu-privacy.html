Privacy Policy

Family Menu by Something Harry LLC
Last updated: November 2026

Family Menu helps a household keep its recipes, plan dinners, vote on what to eat and rate meals. It is built so that your family's information stays in your family's own iCloud account. We don't run servers that store your data, and we don't sell, share or analyze it.

What we collect

Nothing. Something Harry does not collect personal information, usage analytics or advertising identifiers. Family Menu contains no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Where your family's data lives

  • Your iCloud. Recipes, photos, meals, ratings, votes, plans and family members are stored with Apple's CloudKit in the iCloud account of the person who set up the family, and shared with the family members they invite. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. Something Harry cannot see this data.
  • Notifications. To send "How was dinner?" and voting notifications without a server, the app writes a small notification record to the app's CloudKit public database. It contains a random family identifier, a random member identifier, the sender's first name and the dish name. It is used only to deliver the notification.
  • Invite codes. When you create a family, the app stores the family name, a random identifier and the iCloud share link under your 6-character family code, so family members can join by typing the code (for example on Apple Watch).
  • On your device. The app keeps a local copy of your family's data so it opens instantly and works offline.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from a link or share a web page to Family Menu, your device downloads that page directly to read the recipe. We don't see which pages you import.

Children

Family Menu is designed for families, including children who rate meals on an iPhone or Apple Watch. Because we don't collect any personal information, we don't collect information from children. A child's first name and ratings are stored only in the family's iCloud.

Deleting your data

  • Leave a family: Settings → Leave family on this device removes the family's data from that device.
  • Delete everything: the person whose iCloud holds the family can choose Settings → Delete family from iCloud to permanently delete all recipes, meals, ratings, votes and the invite code.
  • You can also manage iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
