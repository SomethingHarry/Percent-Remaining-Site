Privacy Policy

Family Menu by Something Harry LLC
Last updated: November 2026

Family Menu helps a household keep its recipes, plan dinners, vote on what to eat and rate meals. It is built so that your family's information stays in your family's own iCloud account. We don't run servers that store your data, and we never sell or share it.

What we collect

No personal information. Something Harry does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, locations or advertising identifiers. Family Menu contains no ads and no tracking.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn which features families use and what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage events, such as "a dinner was rated" or "a vote was started", to PostHog, an analytics service based in the United States. An event says what happened, plus a few counts (for example how many people are in the family or how many stars a dinner got), the app version and the device type.

  • Events never include names, dish or recipe names, comments, photos, family data, iCloud identifiers or invite codes.
  • Events are tied only to a random identifier the app creates, not to you or your Apple Account. We don't build profiles, and the data is not used for advertising or shared with data brokers.
  • Sample-data mode sends no usage events.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.

Where your family's data lives

  • Your iCloud. Recipes, photos, meals, ratings, votes, plans and family members are stored with Apple's CloudKit in the iCloud account of the person who set up the family, and shared with the family members they invite. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. Something Harry cannot see this data.
  • Notifications. To send "How was dinner?" and voting notifications without a server, the app writes a small notification record to the app's CloudKit public database. It contains a random family identifier, a random member identifier, the sender's first name and the dish name. It is used only to deliver the notification.
  • Invite codes. When you create a family, the app stores the family name, a random identifier and the iCloud share link under your 6-character family code, so family members can join by typing the code (for example on Apple Watch).
  • On your device. The app keeps a local copy of your family's data so it opens instantly and works offline.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from a link or share a web page to Family Menu, your device downloads that page directly to read the recipe. We don't see which pages you import.

Children

Family Menu is designed for families, including children who rate meals on an iPhone or Apple Watch. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't collect personal information from children; usage events are anonymous and never include a child's name or ratings. A child's first name and ratings are stored only in the family's iCloud.

Deleting your data

  • Leave a family: Settings → Leave family on this device removes the family's data from that device.
  • Delete everything: the person whose iCloud holds the family can choose Settings → Delete family from iCloud to permanently delete all recipes, meals, ratings, votes and the invite code.
  • You can also manage iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
