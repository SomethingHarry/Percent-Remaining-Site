Frequently asked questions

How do I set up my family?

Open Family Menu, tap Start a new family, add everyone at your table and tap Create. Then go to Settings → Invite family and send the invite link. Each person taps the link (or enters the family code) and picks their name.

Can someone use it with only an Apple Watch?

Yes. Install Family Menu on the watch from the App Store on the watch, open it, type your family code and pick your name. Ratings and votes work right from the watch and its notifications.

How do weekly plans and votes work?

In the Plan tab, a meal maker or the family owner can ask everyone to vote 👍 or 👎 on a set of dinners for the week, then build the week from the winners, or fill open days with family favorites.

When do people get asked to rate dinner?

For planned dinners, everyone gets "How was dinner?" at the family's rating time, and whoever cooked gets a recap at the recap time. Change both in Settings → Follow-ups.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Family Menu, that you're signed in to iCloud, and that you picked your own name in the app.

How do I delete my family's data?

The person whose iCloud holds the family can choose Settings → Delete family from iCloud. Anyone can remove the family from their own device with Settings → Leave family on this device.
