Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2025

Joyscroll values your privacy. Your personal memories belong to you, and this app is designed so your photos and videos never leave your device.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or transmit your personal photos or videos. All photo access happens locally on your device, and Joyscroll never uploads your library to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Joyscroll uses:

  • AdMob to show occasional ads. AdMob may collect limited device data for ad delivery, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck to collect anonymized usage analytics. This data is aggregate-only and helps us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Your Choices

You may choose to grant Limited Photo Access or Full Photo Access in iOS Settings. You can change these permissions at any time by visiting: Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Joyscroll.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, or wish to request more details, please email us at support@joyscroll.app.

Thank you for trusting Joyscroll to help you relive your memories!
